MAY – JUNE 2027 · DELIVERY

BVI → Azores → Mediterranean

Start in the British Virgin Islands, cross the Atlantic via the Azores, then continue toward Gibraltar and the Mediterranean. This phase is primarily a delivery passage with long offshore legs and weather-dependent stops.

JULY – AUGUST 2027 · MEDITERRANEAN

Albania · Greece · Turkey

Summer in the eastern Mediterranean with a focus on anchorages, islands and longer stays. The exact route between Albania, Greece and Turkey remains deliberately flexible.

SEPTEMBER – OCTOBER 2027 · WESTBOUND

Eastern Mediterranean → Gibraltar

A gradual move back west. Which coasts and islands we include depends on weather, crew and the best available windows. The goal of this phase is Gibraltar and the gateway back to the Atlantic.

Gibraltar → Canaries → Azores → BVI

Atlantic phase with the Canary Islands and Azores as planned stops, followed by the major crossing back to the Caribbean. Order, timing and stops remain dependent on weather and the final season plan.
