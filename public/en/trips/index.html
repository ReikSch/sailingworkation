WHAT'S INCLUDED

What the trip price includes

Included: your booked berth or cabin, the boat, skippers, boat insurance, bed linen and towels.
Paid separately through the shared onboard fund: approx. €200 per person/week for food, diesel, gas, marina and mooring fees. Travel to and from the boat, personal expenses and individual travel insurance are not included.

Payment

50% of the trip price is due with the booking confirmation. The remaining 50% is due 30 days before the trip starts.

Application before booking

An inquiry is not yet a booking. Because we will live together in a small crew for several weeks, we put each crew together deliberately. After your inquiry, we contact you personally, clarify open questions and make sure the trip is a good fit for both sides. Your place is only confirmed once we have accepted the booking and received the 50% deposit.

Cancellation

More than 60 days before departure: 20% of the trip price · 60–31 days before departure: 50% · from 30 days before departure: 100%. A replacement person nominated by the guest cannot automatically take over the booking because we select the crew personally. If we are able to refill the place ourselves with a suitable guest, we may voluntarily reduce the cancellation charge accordingly.
