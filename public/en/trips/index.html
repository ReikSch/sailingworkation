The broad season route is set. What makes these legs Workation-friendly is the rhythm: stable morning work windows, movement later in the day and enough buffer for weather and real jobs.

WORKATION SEASON ROUTE · 2027/28BVI → Azores → Mediterranean
The highlighted section follows your scroll position. Exact daily tracks remain weather-dependent; work-friendly timing is planned around stable morning work windows.
01
MAY – JUNE 2027 · DELIVERY

BVI → Azores → Mediterranean

Start in the British Virgin Islands, cross the Atlantic via the Azores, then continue toward Gibraltar and the Mediterranean. This phase is primarily a delivery passage with long offshore legs and weather-dependent stops.

JULY – AUGUST 2027 · MEDITERRANEAN

Albania · Greece · Turkey

Summer in the eastern Mediterranean with a focus on anchorages, islands and longer stays. The exact route between Albania, Greece and Turkey remains deliberately flexible.

AnchoringIslandsFlexible
03
SEPTEMBER – OCTOBER 2027 · WESTBOUND

Eastern Mediterranean → Gibraltar

A gradual move back west. Which coasts and islands we include depends on weather, crew and the best available windows. The goal of this phase is Gibraltar and the gateway back to the Atlantic.

MediterraneanWestboundWeather windows
04
OCTOBER – NOVEMBER/DECEMBER 2027 · ATLANTIC

Gibraltar → Canaries → Cape Verde → BVI

From Gibraltar we head south-west to the Canary Islands, continue to Cape Verde and then cross to the British Virgin Islands. This is the least typical Workation leg: longer offshore passages mean work windows depend more heavily on watch schedules and conditions.

05
DECEMBER 2027 – FEBRUARY 2028 · CARIBBEAN

British Virgin Islands

Winter base in the BVIs: short distances, many anchorages and much more time in individual places. An ideal phase for longer stays without constant passage pressure.
