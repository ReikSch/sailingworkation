01 · THE IDEA

Not a holiday with a laptop.
A workation that actually works.

You shouldn't have to choose between doing good work and seeing more of the world. Sailing Workation is for remote workers who want something beyond the usual coworking-space-and-Airbnb routine.

02 · YOUR DAY

Work. Sail. Afterwork.

A clear rhythm that takes your work seriously — and the sea just as seriously.

03 · WORK SETUP

The boat needs
to work as an office.

A beautiful bay doesn't help when your call drops or your battery dies. That's why the boat is planned for remote work from day one.

01

Starlink aboard

Satellite internet as the core setup, backed up by local mobile networks where useful.

02

Power all day

Solar, a large battery bank, generator and 230-V power are planned to keep work independent from shore power.

03

More than one desk

Salon, cockpit and deck instead of a fixed desk. Pick your workspace based on calls, sun and wind.

04

Work before movement

No daily relocation just for the sake of moving. If important work is happening, we simply stay at anchor longer.

04 · LIVE ABOARD
