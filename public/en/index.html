01 · A WORKDAY THAT MOVES

Productive mornings.
Moving afternoons.

We do not build the workday around sailing — we structure it so both can work. Important calls and deep work get reliable time windows. Afterwards the office becomes a boat again.

08:00—13:0001

WORK

Calls, focus, coffee, ocean view. Quiet at anchor instead of rushing between marinas.

02 · THE INFRASTRUCTURE

The view is a bonus.
The connection is essential.

01

Starlink

Satellite internet as the primary connection, backed up by local mobile networks where they perform better.

02

Power

Solar, a large battery bank, generator and 230-V power are planned to keep work independent from shore power.

03

Workspaces

Salon, cockpit and deck instead of a fixed desk. Switch your workspace based on calls, sun and wind.

We do not plan to move every day. Work and weather get the buffer they need.

06 · FAQ

Remote work,
just with more sea.

Can I do real calls and meetings?

That is exactly what the concept is designed for. Starlink and sufficient power are core parts of the planned setup. Weather, connectivity and technology can still never be guaranteed 100% at sea.

Do we sail every afternoon?

No. Sometimes the best workspace is a quiet anchorage. If work, weather or crew make it sensible, we stay longer.

How many guests are aboard?

Regulär maximal sechs Gäste plus Skipper-Team. Dadurch bleiben drei guest cabins verfügbar.

What if I want to switch off completely?

Then Sailing Sabbatical is the better fit: less work structure, longer passages and a more intentional break.
