SKIPPER & FOUNDER OF SAILING SABBATICAL AND SAILING WORKATION

“Freedom means setting my own course.”

At the end of 2025, I left my secure job as a team leader and left Germany. Not because I already had a perfect plan, but because I never wanted to look back one day and wonder why I kept postponing my dreams.

I wanted to get out, discover the world and find out what it really feels like to live on my own terms.

Today I spend much of my time on sailing boats. There's hardly a better feeling for me than waking up in the morning and knowing that home is wherever we're anchored. Sometimes we sail on to the next island; sometimes we stay for a few more days. That's exactly the freedom I love.

As a person, I'm fairly quiet and reserved. I don't need to be the centre of attention. I prefer listening and value honest conversations. As a skipper, though, my job also means taking responsibility. When decisions have to be made, I try to keep a cool head, stay calm and help the crew feel secure.

By now I've sailed several thousand nautical miles on different boats and in different areas, including longer deliveries and offshore passages, and I work as a skipper. I hold the German SKS sailing certificate and SRC radio certificate and am preparing for the SSS. Even so, I know there's always more to learn at sea.

With Sailing Sabbatical and Sailing Workation, Sebastian and I want to create something beyond an ordinary sailing holiday. I want to share my love of sailing, give people opportunities to experience something new and perhaps encourage a few of them not to keep putting their own dreams on hold.

One thing I've learnt along the way is that we often need less than we think. But sometimes we also need the courage to simply set off.

SKIPPER & FOUNDER OF SAILING SABBATICAL AND SAILING WORKATION

“Adventurer, free spirit & team catalyst”

Ahoy, everyone!

I'm Sebastian — or rather: Buddy. I have my younger brother Julian to thank for that nickname. As a child, he couldn't quite pronounce my name, so he simply came up with his own version. It stuck, and it's been with me ever since. Thanks for that, Julian!

I've always loved sport, and I'm happiest where the sea begins and the horizon seems to have no limits.

I discovered sailing while studying sports science. My first experiences were in dinghies and sport catamarans, and from then on I was hooked.

After completing a second degree in business psychology and gaining my first professional experience, I spent several years in Australia. It was a time of travel, new friendships and unforgettable experiences that broadened my horizons and taught me one thing above all: I belong by the sea. Or better yet, right in it — where the sun shines, life happens outdoors and, for a moment, everything feels right.

During the unusual pandemic years, I started turning my dream of living on the water into a real plan. I took my German powerboat licence and then the SKS sailing certificate. It was during the SKS course that I met Reik. We quickly realised we shared not only a passion for sailing but the same bigger dream. Since the end of 2024, we've been using every opportunity to get out on the water together and move one step closer to it.

When I'm not at the helm, I'm an all-round water sports enthusiast. Wakeboarding, waterskiing, kitesurfing, surfing, SUP, snorkelling or spearfishing — as long as it involves being on or in the water, I'm in. And the fishing rod comes along too: we have high-quality offshore fishing equipment aboard for longer fishing trips.

Music matters to me just as much as sport. Even in the most remote anchorage, the soundtrack has to be right. And when the crew is in the mood, a quiet bay can turn into a little dance floor beneath the open sky.

If you're after something calmer, you'll find me cooking, playing chess, backgammon, Wizard or cards, or enjoying an outdoor movie night under the stars. We've even got a projector aboard for that.

Above all, the sense of community aboard matters to me. I love cooking, looking after the crew and making sure everyone feels at home. I believe a great time isn't made by beautiful places alone, but by the people you share them with.

My motto: We are family — and that's how we treat each other.

My big dream is to sail around the world on our own boat: discovering new countries, getting to know different cultures and, eventually, losing track of what day of the week it is. To me, that's living the good life.

Still have questions? Ask us →
How reliable is the internet?

Starlink is central to our Workation concept, particularly while anchored. Availability depends on location, tariff, conditions and equipment. On open-sea passages we cannot promise uninterrupted connections or fixed online working hours; we will discuss suitability for your job before confirming a leg.

Can I do video calls and meetings?

On suitable Workation legs, yes – especially while anchored in good conditions. We cannot guarantee video calls during ocean crossings, long offshore passages or severe weather. Please mention any mandatory meetings in your enquiry.

When do we work?

The preferred work window is in the morning and late morning. This makes the day easier to plan for the whole crew and leaves more afternoons available for sailing or changing anchorage. You can of course continue working later, but you should not expect the entire boat to remain silent around you.

Will there be proper workspaces aboard?

Yes. On the Lagoon 46 “My Elli”, the saloon, cockpit and other usable areas provide space for laptop work and video calls. Depending on sun, wind and how much quiet you need, your workspace can move around the boat.

What happens in bad weather or if connectivity fails?

In bad weather we stay in a protected location and adapt the route to safe conditions. We do not expect major connectivity issues with Starlink, but exceptional weather or technical problems can still cause temporary limitations.

Do we sail every afternoon?

No. Sometimes the best workspace is a quiet anchorage. If work, weather or crew make it sensible, we stay longer. The Workation needs to work first – it is not about maximising nautical miles.

Is the Workation suitable for employees?

Yes, provided your employer explicitly allows you to work from the relevant country during that period. Any necessary approval from your employer is your responsibility.

What is included in the price?

Your berth or booked cabin, the skippers, bed linen and towels are included. The boat and boat insurance are included as well. Starlink is charged separately depending on region, tariff and actual usage.

What is paid from the onboard kitty?

Food, diesel, gas, marina or mooring fees and other shared running costs are paid from the onboard kitty. Guests organise and manage the onboard kitty between themselves; no payments from it go to us. Personal spending and individual travel insurance are not included.

Can I book on my own?

Yes. Booking one berth in a double cabin is the standard option. A full double cabin for single occupancy can be booked with an 85% surcharge. Two people booking together automatically share one cabin.

What is life aboard like?

Think of it like a good shared flat: we live together, cook together and share responsibility for the boat. This is not an all-inclusive charter. Shore time and personal activities are of course up to you.

Do I have to help with sailing?

No previous experience is required, but an interest in sailing is very welcome. When both skippers are aboard, we can operate the boat without guest assistance if necessary. Still, taking part is part of the concept, and many guests actively want to learn.

How do you handle alcohol aboard?

This is not a party trip. During the week, the work rhythm comes first, so alcohol is more likely to be part of weekends or free evenings. Responsible consumption is generally fine.

Where do we embark and disembark?

We do not plan around one fixed home port and prefer to anchor. Depending on the leg, the crew may therefore be picked up by dinghy from a beach or another suitable meeting point. Exact locations will be shared before each trip.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes. Guests must be at least 18 years old.

Is smoking allowed aboard?

Smoking is allowed only in designated outdoor areas. Smoking inside cabins or interior spaces is not permitted.

Can I bring a pet?

No. We cannot accommodate pets aboard.

Do I need my own travel insurance?

Yes. Each guest is responsible for arranging suitable travel insurance, international health insurance and, if desired, trip cancellation cover.

What if I want to switch off completely?

Then Sailing Sabbatical is probably the better fit: less work structure, longer passages and a more intentional break.
