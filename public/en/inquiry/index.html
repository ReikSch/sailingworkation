Application before booking

An inquiry is not yet a booking. Because we will live together in a small crew for several weeks, we put each crew together deliberately. After your inquiry, we contact you personally, clarify open questions and make sure the trip is a good fit for both sides. Your place is only confirmed once we have accepted the booking and received the 50% deposit.

Payment & cancellation

Once we have accepted your booking, 50% of the trip price is due as a deposit and the remaining 50% is due 30 days before departure. Cancellation charges are 20% more than 60 days before departure, 50% from 60–31 days before departure and 100% from 30 days before departure. A replacement guest nominated by you cannot automatically take over the place because we select the crew personally. If we are able to refill the place ourselves with a suitable guest, we may voluntarily reduce the cancellation charge accordingly.
