SLOW SAILING

One year. Twelve experiences.

From the BVIs we head south through the Lesser Antilles. In May, guests join us for the passage to Bonaire; then we take our time through the ABC islands, Colombia and San Blas towards Bocas del Toro. None of the proposed trips exceeds 520 nautical miles, and most cover far less.

The philosophy

Two to five weeks aboard, fewer moves, more time for people and places. Apply for one leg or combine consecutive legs to stay longer.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Slow sailing. Clear priorities.

South for hurricane season

Our target is to reach Bonaire by late May. The ABC islands and southern Caribbean face historically lower, but not zero, hurricane risk. Weather monitoring and safe-harbour contingencies are essential.

Passages are part of the trip

Guests can join the Grenada–Bonaire and Aruba–Colombia passages. Longer sea legs, possible night watches and weather buffers make them Sabbatical-focused voyages.

Honest workation expectations

Workation trips prioritise long anchor stays and work windows. Internet, video calls and power remain location- and weather-dependent; we check your requirements before acceptance.
